New in this version (0.6.0 and 0.6.1 are withdrawn: they could not play untagged files):
- Favorites: a heart on tracks, albums and in the player bar; a Favorites tile in the grid.
- Albums show their genre; the search finds genre and composer.
- Albums sort by their sort names, or ignoring a leading "The".
- Tracks play at the level their ReplayGain or iTunes Sound Check tags describe; an album keeps its dynamics.
- Targets Android 17; the themed launcher icon matches the colour icon.
