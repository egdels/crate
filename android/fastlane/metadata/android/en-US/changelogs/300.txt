First release on Google Play.

Crate plays the music files you already have: MP3, FLAC, M4A, M4B, WAV, Ogg and Opus. Pick a folder, Crate reads the tags where the files are and shows your albums with covers. Gapless playback, lock screen and headset controls, search, two panes on tablets.

No account, no cloud, no ads, no network permission. The privacy policy is in the app's menu.

Free software under GPL-3.0: https://github.com/egdels/crate
